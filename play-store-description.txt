Fintraq is a manual personal finance ledger for daily expenses, income, transfers, and money lent or borrowed. Track what is owed by person and keep it alongside your accounts—without a Fintraq sign-up or a linked bank.

TRACK EVERYDAY MONEY
• Record expenses, income, and account-to-account transfers
• Organize transactions by account and category
• See balances across cash, bank, wallet, and other accounts
• Track multiple currencies
• Review spending summaries and recent activity

KEEP TRACK OF MONEY OWED
Record money you lend or borrow against a person. See outstanding balances and repayments, and set reminders for due dates or loan payments.

USE IT OFFLINE
Your local ledger works without an internet connection. You do not need to connect a bank account or create a Fintraq account to track transactions.

FINTRAQ PRO — ONE-TIME PURCHASE
Pro unlocks extended analytics, global search, CSV export, and optional Google Drive backup and restore. Google Drive backup requires sign-in and an internet connection. Pro is a one-time purchase; there is no subscription.
